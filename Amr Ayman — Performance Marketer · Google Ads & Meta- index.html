What I Do

A look at the work behind the numbers.

I work as a hands-on media buyer, not an account manager who hands off the work. I plan the structure, write the ads, set up the tracking, run the budget and report on it every month.

In Saudi Arabia, my focus is Google Search for service businesses, where one qualified enquiry is worth far more than a click. In Egypt, I run paid social for online stores, where the target is profitable sales.

My background in business administration (currently an MBA candidate at Ain Shams University) and a business I founded and ran myself mean I look at ad spend the way an owner does: what comes back for every riyal or pound.

  • Restructured a legal-services account with 100+ campaigns, removing keywords that were competing against each other.
  • Built branch-level Search campaigns for Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam, plus a general brand campaign.
  • Set up GA4 and Google Tag Manager conversion tracking so spend is judged by enquiries.
  • Delivered strategy decks and monthly reporting to clients, including an 11-slide strategy deck for a law firm.
  • Analyzed paid and organic performance for a fashion brand and found budget going to weaker campaigns while stronger ones were paused.
  • Built a phased media-buying plan for a Saudi handmade-products brand across multiple platforms.
  • Managed Meta campaigns for e-commerce stores, with EGP 5M+ in cumulative ad spend.

The situation

The account had 100+ historical campaigns overlapping on the same keywords, so they competed against each other and spend was hard to read by branch.

What I did
  • Restructured the account by branch: Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam
  • Added a general and brand campaign
  • Set up conversion tracking for enquiries
  • Reviewed search terms and cut wasted spend
The result

Conversions grew from 149 in July (first weeks with tracking live) to 706 in September at SAR 54 each. Over the full period: 1,951 conversions on SAR 135.9K.

Egyptian men’s fashion brand · paid, organic and website sales

+15%Sales vs. prior period

Store-wide results during my engagement, not attributed to a single campaign or channel.

The situation

The brand had strong campaigns running next to weak ones, with no clear view of what was driving sales.

What I did
  • Analyzed organic content, messaging ads and website sales
  • Found strong campaigns paused while weaker ones kept spending
  • Found photo posts outperforming Reels, with Friday and Saturday as peak days
  • Flagged an unexplained mid-period order spike for investigation
The result

Findings delivered as clear decks with specific changes: reallocating budget to the better-performing campaigns, and a content calendar built around the strongest days and formats.
